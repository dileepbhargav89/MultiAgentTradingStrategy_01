1. Quantitative Overview & Thesis

StrategyOne is an autonomous, multi-agent trading system engineered for high-liquidity cryptocurrency spot and perpetual futures markets. The core philosophy resolves a fundamental flaw in traditional quantitative crypto strategies: monolithic models overtrade in choppy sideways markets, dissipating capital across false breakouts.
